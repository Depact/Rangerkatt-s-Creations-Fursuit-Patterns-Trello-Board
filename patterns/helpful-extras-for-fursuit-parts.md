<a id="free-rangerkatt-cuff-tutorial"></a>

### (FREE) Rangerkatt Cuff Tutorial     

Using a sewing machine is best but technically could be done with a backstitch.

If you’re NOT sewing a liner, this can be done BEFORE adding the toes/fingers to the wrist/ankle.

Otherwise do this last.

‌

1. Measure the length needed for your cuff, added an extra 2-3 inches to the length. I prefer a wide cuff and make it at least 3 inches wide (you’ll be able to cut off extra at the end).
2. Sew with the cuff material (minky, fleece, etc) lined up with the edge of the fur wrist. (This is just easiest, if you want, you can pin it wherever you’d like your cuff to start.) Have the nice side of the fabric facing the fur. You're sewing right side out first.
3. Pin together, I prefer the pins at a right angle to the edge of the materials.
4. Use a straight stitch, however far from the edge of the wrist you sew will set how wide your cuff is.
5. As you get to the end, remove however much extra cuff material you have and be sure to reverse over you final stitches.
6. (Optional) Cut off the extra bulk cuff material between the edge of the paw and where you just sewed.
7. Flip your paws inside out and sew the cuff from the inside. These stitches should be hidden within the fur on the other side.
8. Flip paws back to right-side out and brush out fur from seams.
9. Done! For an even more seamless look, ladder stitch where the cuff meets itself.
