Womens' Full Form:

Tired of making a DTD? Want something more reusable?

Input your measurements (in both tabs for a more accurate form) and get a custom dress form! Men, women, and plus sizes available. Torso, arms and legs are separate patterns, simple sew together for a full dummy.

Be sure to choose whether you want your pattern to come with seam allowance or not!
